# Icons

Outline icons on a 24px grid, 1.75px stroke, round caps and joins, drawn in `currentColor`. They ship on `window.Areeba.icons`; render one with `h(Areeba.icons.card, { width: 24, height: 24 })`. Icons never stand alone for destructive actions: pair them with a label.
