# Writing amounts and errors

People act on money, so every amount must be impossible to misread and every error must say what to do next. These rules apply in English and Arabic; the Arabic column is what ships, not a translation afterthought.

## Amounts

| Rule | English | Arabic |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Currency symbol or code before the number, no space for symbols | `$12,480.50` · `LBP 1,500,000` | `$12,480.50` · `LBP 1,500,000` (kept left-to-right) |
| Two decimals for USD and EUR; none for LBP | `$42.00`, not `$42` | same digits |
| Debits use the minus sign `−` (U+2212), credits `+` | `−$20.90` · `+$1,250.00` | `−$20.90` · `+$1,250.00` |
| Digits are always 0–9 | `14:32`, `30 Nov` | `14:32`، `30 نوفمبر` (never ١٤:٣٢) |
| Thousands with commas, decimal point | `$186,420` | `$186,420` |
| An amount inside a sentence is isolated | `Transfer sent: $250.00` | ``` `تم إرسال ${ltr('$250.00')}` ``` |

- **Never truncate an amount.** Shorten the label instead, or wrap.
- **Spending is ink, not red.** Red means something went wrong; a payment is not an error. Only credits get `success` green, with their `+`.
- **Amounts, IBANs, card and reference numbers stay left-to-right in Arabic.** Components that show amounts do this for you (ListItem, DataTable, DetailList with `ltr`, AmountInput). For text you write, wrap the amount with `Areeba.ltr()`; without it the browser shows `12,480.50$`.
- **Big numbers in dashboards** may use `k` and `M` (`$1.24M`), never in balances, receipts or confirmations.
- **Dates:** "Today, 14:32", "12 Nov", "12 Nov 2025" when not this year. 24-hour time. Arabic month names come from `Intl`; the digits stay 0–9.

## Errors

An error has two parts: **what happened**, then **what to do**. Write it for the person, not the system.

| Situation | English | Arabic |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Card declined | Your bank declined the payment. Try another card or contact your bank. | رفض مصرفك الدفع. جرّب بطاقة أخرى أو تواصل مع مصرفك. |
| Field too short | This IBAN is too short. Check the 28 characters and try again. | رقم الآيبان قصير جدًا. تحقق من الخانات الـ 28 وحاول مجددًا. |
| Over a limit | More than your daily limit of $5,000.00. Enter a smaller amount. | أكثر من حدّك اليومي البالغ $5,000.00. أدخل مبلغًا أقل. |
| Wrong code | That code is not right. Check it and try again. | الرمز غير صحيح. تحقق منه وحاول مجددًا. |
| No connection | We couldn’t reach areeba. Check your connection; nothing was charged. | تعذّر الوصول إلى areeba. تحقق من اتصالك؛ لم يُخصم أي مبلغ. |
| Missing choice | Choose the type of document you are uploading. | اختر نوع المستند الذي تحمّله. |

- **Say whether money moved.** After a failed payment, always say if anything was charged: "No money was taken."
- **No blame, no codes first.** "Something went wrong (E1042)" helps no one. Put a reference after the fix, if support needs it: "Reference TXN-2026-118204".
- **No "please", "sorry" or "oops".** Be direct and calm. "Invalid" and "error" are not explanations.
- **Use the same words as the UI.** If the button says "Top up", the error says "top up", not "recharge".

### Where errors go

- **Under the field** (`error` on TextField, TextArea, Select, RadioGroup, FileUpload): for one field. It replaces the hint, turns the field `danger` and is announced.
- **An Alert at the top of the form**: when several fields fail, say how many ("Check 3 fields") and mark each field.
- **A Result screen**: when a whole flow fails (payment declined).
- **A Toast**: only for something that failed in the background, with a retry action.
- **Never colour alone.** Every error has words; every status has a word and an icon.

## Hints

Put the rules in the hint **before** people type or upload, so the error rarely appears: "28 characters, starts with LB", "PDF, JPG or PNG, up to 10 MB". The placeholder is an example, never the label or the rule.
