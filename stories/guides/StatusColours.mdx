# Status colours

Status is always **a word, an icon or a dot, and a colour**, in that order of importance. Colour alone fails people who cannot see it, and screen readers do not announce it.

## The four status colours

| Tone | Means | Words to use | Never for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `success` | Done, money arrived, safe to move on | Completed, Paid, Accepted, Verified, Live | Decoration, brand moments |
| `warning` | Waiting, or needs attention soon | Pending, Expires soon, Close to your limit | Errors that block |
| `danger` | Failed, declined, or blocks the next step | Declined, Failed, Not accepted, Locked | Spending, debits, the brand |
| `info` | Neutral news, in progress | In transit, Scheduled, New | Anything people must act on |
| `neutral` | Finished but not good or bad | Refunded, Expired, Archived, Draft | — |

## Rules

- **Brand is not a status.** Crimson Deep (`brand`) is for actions and selection; Signal Red (`accent`) is only the logo and headline highlights. Neither ever marks a status.
- **Spending is ink.** A debit is normal, so it stays `ink` with its `−`. Only credits use `success`, with their `+`.
- **Soft backgrounds for surfaces, solid colour for marks.** Alerts and badges sit on the `-soft` tint with the tone's text colour (all pass 4.5:1 in light and dark); dots, icons and meter fills use the solid tone.
- **Pick the strongest true tone, once.** A declined payment is `danger` in the list badge, the details sheet and the Result screen, not `warning` in one place and `danger` in another.
- **Charts never use status colours** for series; they use `chart-1` to `chart-4`.
- **Meters** turn `warning` from 75% and `danger` from 90% of a limit; set `success` for progress people want to finish.

## Where status appears

- **Badge** in lists and tables: one or two words.
- **Alert** above content: a title and what to do.
- **Toast** after an action: what happened, with undo where possible.
- **Result** at the end of a flow: success or failure, with the facts.
- **NotificationItem status**: how a request ended (Accepted, Declined, Expired).
