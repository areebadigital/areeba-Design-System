# ActionBar

The sticky bottom area of a flow step that holds its actions: Continue, Confirm and top up, Share.

- One primary action, full width, `size-control-lg` (48px) as for mobile primaries. A secondary goes beside it, or below it with `stacked`.
- The primary repeats the outcome: "Confirm and top up $100.00", not "OK".
- It sticks to the bottom of the screen and adds the safe-area inset; place it after the screen's main content.

From the areeba pay app, restyled to the system tokens.
