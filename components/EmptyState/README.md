# EmptyState

What a screen shows when there is nothing yet: first run, no results, or cleared lists.

- Title says what's missing; body says why it matters or what will appear; one action gets them started.
- For "no results" after a search or filter, drop the illustration and offer "Clear filters".
- The illustration is token-coloured shapes (`mint`, `brand-soft`, `brand`); swap for brand illustrations when available.
